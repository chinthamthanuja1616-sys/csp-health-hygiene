Objectives

What our Health & Hygiene project aims to achieve.

🧼

Hygiene Awareness

Teach simple daily practices such as hand washing, bathing and keeping nails and clothes clean.

💧

Clean Water and Sanitation

Understand safe drinking water, water storage and sanitation in homes and public places.

📚

Health Education

Share reliable health information with children and families in an easy, friendly way.

🏘️

Building a Healthy Community

Encourage everyone to work together for clean surroundings and healthier lives.
