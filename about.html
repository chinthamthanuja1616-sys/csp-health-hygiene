About the Project

Who we are, where we worked and what we set out to do.

Our Community Service Project focuses on personal hygiene, healthy living, clean surroundings, safe drinking water and community awareness. By visiting a primary health centre, households and a school in Inamadugu and Kovur, we learned how everyday habits protect a whole community, and shared that knowledge with children and families.
