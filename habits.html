Healthy Habits

Simple daily habits that keep families and communities healthy.

Hand Washing

Wash hands with soap for 20 seconds before eating and after using the toilet.

Healthy Food

Eat fresh fruits, vegetables and home-cooked meals, and drink plenty of safe water.

Clean Surroundings

Keep homes, schools and streets free of waste and stagnant water to prevent disease.

Health Awareness

Know your nearest health centre, get check-ups and follow advice from health workers.
